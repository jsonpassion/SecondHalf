1. 서비스 소개

Second Half(이하 "앱")는 새 TOEFL iBT를 준비하는 한국어 사용자가 읽기 유형 Complete the Words에 대비해 학술 어휘의 철자를 익히도록 돕는, 독립적으로 개발된 iOS 앱입니다. 앱은 TOEFL을 시행하는 ETS와 관계가 없습니다. 앱은 네 밴드의 학술 예문 카드 3,855장, 모른 단어를 입력해 확인하는 직접 쓰기, 어미가 만드는 품사와 그 적중률을 정리한 어미 노트를 제공합니다. 처음 7일(공부한 날 기준)은 제한 없이 무료이고, 그 뒤에는 하루 이용량 안에서 무료이며, 구독(이용권)으로 하루 제한을 없앨 수 있습니다.

English

1. About the service

Second Half ("the App") is an independently developed iOS application that helps Korean speakers preparing for the new TOEFL iBT learn to spell academic words for the reading task Complete the Words. The App is not affiliated with ETS, which administers the TOEFL. The App provides 3,855 academic sentence cards in four bands, a typing check for the words you did not know, and ending notes that show which part of speech an ending makes and how often. The first seven study days are free without limit; after that the App stays free within a daily allowance, and a subscription (a pass) removes the daily limit.

2. Free use and in-app purchases

  • Free: the first seven days on which you study are free without limit. After that, 20 cards and 10 typed words a day stay free, with every band and feature still available. Clearing the study record does not restart the free week, and on the same iCloud account it carries over a reinstall.
  • Paid: a pass is a monthly subscription or a yearly subscription; either removes the daily limit on every card of all four bands. The scope of a pass you have already bought is never reduced.
  • Auto-renewing subscriptions (monthly, yearly): unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, a subscription renews for the same period at the same price, and your Apple account is charged within 24 hours before the period ends. You can cancel at any time in iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, and you keep access until the paid period ends. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
  • Price changes: subscription price changes are announced in advance under Apple's policies, and an increase is not applied to existing subscribers without their consent.
  • Prices are those shown in the App Store and may vary by country, exchange rate and promotion.
  • Payment is handled through your Apple account; no sign-up or login is required.
  • Purchases can be restored with "Restore purchases" on another device signed in to the same Apple account, or after reinstalling the App.
  • Refunds, including subscription refunds, follow the Apple App Store's refund policy. The Developer cannot process refunds directly; request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

3. Study data

Your band, rounds, per-card records and typing list are stored on your device and in your own iCloud key-value storage. The Developer collects or stores none of your study data on any server. "Clear study record" in the App clears the whole record and cannot be undone.

4. Content, ending notes and voice

  • Every word, sentence and voice clip is inside the App; studying needs no internet connection. Content may be added or corrected in App updates.
  • The voice that reads words and sentences was not recorded by a person; it was made in advance with an AI voice model. Sentences without a clip are read by the device's built-in voice.
  • The cards are practice sentences written by the Developer after the way the Complete the Words task is set; they are not actual test questions.
  • The parts of speech and rates in the ending notes were measured by the Developer over the words in the App. Endings have exceptions, and results may differ for other words or contexts.
  • The bands follow the 1-6 band and 0-120 concordance published by ETS for reference; they are not an official score or judgment.
  • TOEFL® is a registered trademark of ETS. "TOEFL" is used in the App and its store listing only to say which test the content prepares for; the App is not affiliated with, sponsored, endorsed or approved by ETS.

5. Disclaimer

The App is a study aid and does not guarantee any band or score. Round records and ending-note rates are practice aids.
